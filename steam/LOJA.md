## Short description

**English**

> A 1-bit action metroidvania inside an infected computer. You are a sniffer dog with a debugger for a blaster: run, dash and shoot through folders, sniff out hidden files and buried clues, break quarantines, and delete the Ghost File haunting the Trash.

**Português (Brasil)**

> Um metroidvania de ação 1-bit dentro de um computador infectado. Você é um cão farejador armado com um debugger: corra, dê dash e atire entre pastas, fareje arquivos ocultos e pistas enterradas, rompa quarentenas e exclua o Arquivo Fantasma que assombra a Lixeira.

## About this game

**English**

[h2]Good dog. Bad files.[/h2]
Something is corrupting the computer, and the hound is the process sent to hunt it down. Windows are platforms, folders are rooms and the bugs are very real.

[list]
[*][b]Shoot fast, sniff smart.[/b] Sniffing slows time, exposes weak points for double damage and reveals what the walls are hiding.
[*][b]Follow your nose.[/b] Hidden files become platforms only when sniffed, and buried clues are the key to the Trash.
[*][b]Break quarantine.[/b] Fight out of locked rooms, dodge diving Spam and open shortcuts home.
[*][b]New tools, new paths.[/b] The dash is also a key: it takes you through lasers and back into places that were closed.
[*][b]Two points of view.[/b] Side-view exploration flips into a top-down twin-stick arena when you go deep into a process.
[*][b]A boss that cheats.[/b] The Ghost File turns intangible, charges and copies itself. Your nose knows which one is real.
[*][b]1-bit with one color.[/b] Black, white and a single accent, with an original chiptune score that tightens as bugs get close.
[*][b]Made for controllers and keyboards.[/b] Both can be remapped, and on-screen prompts follow what you are using. Screen shake can be turned off.
[/list]

## Notas para o analista (build)

> No account, login or internet needed. Keyboard, mouse or gamepad (remappable
> in Options > Controls). Press down in front of a folder to enter it. Sniff
> (Shift / LT) often: it makes hidden platforms solid and unearths clues.
> Route to the boss:
> 1. C:\Documents, first folder: climb C:\Pictures sniffing, clear the Gallery
>    quarantine, sniff at the far right for clue 1, return by the folder.
> 2. Upper-right exit of Documents, through Downloads: climb C:\Temp sniffing,
>    clear the Setup quarantine, take the dash chip and clue 2, return by the folder.
> 3. Dash through the laser in Documents. In C:\Trash take the first folder:
>    cross System32 (dash, sniff the walls), climb Drivers, clear the quarantine,
>    take clue 3, return by the folder.
> 4. Sniff next to the wall at the end of the Trash, clear the 45-second room
>    (keep moving), enter the same door again. In the boss's second half, hold
>    sniff while shooting.
